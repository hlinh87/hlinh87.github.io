CO-AUTHORED STUDY · VIETNAMMARCH – JUNE 2024

Why Consumers Come Back

I co-authored a study about why consumers in Vietnam buy green cosmetics again. I worked through 50+ pages of interview data, using structured coding and thematic analysis to find patterns in what customers said.

The evidence: interviews with 40 regular customers and five experts from the Vietnam Natural Products Science Association.

The result: six key drivers of repurchase intention. This is the kind of question that makes me interested in business analytics.

Consumer behaviorQualitative researchThematic analysis

Publication reference: Factors Influencing Consumers’ Repurchase Intention Green Cosmetics in Vietnam, International Journal of Agriculture and Biological Sciences.
